Soft Orb Lab

A soft, glowing orb. A few hidden control points push and pull its outline, and colored lights drift around inside it, so it flexes and shifts color slowly. It leans toward your cursor. Press and hold to grab it and pull, or click to splash color. Turn on the camera and both hands do the same: pinch to grab, pull both sides apart like putty. See it as a whole homepage →
