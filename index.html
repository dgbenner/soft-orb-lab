Soft Orb Lab

A soft, glowing orb. A few hidden control points push and pull its outline, and colored lights drift around inside it, so it flexes and shifts color slowly. It leans toward your cursor, and clicking splashes a new color where you click. Pick a palette or let it cycle through them. See it as a whole homepage →
